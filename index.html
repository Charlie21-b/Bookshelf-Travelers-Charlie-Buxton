Bookshelf Travelers

A delightful adventure story for ages 4-10.

About the Book

When a bedtime shooting star wish pulls Max and Brody straight into the pages of classic adventure novels, the two best friends must survive harpoon hunts in 20,000 Leagues Under the Sea and pirate encounters in Treasure Island to find their way home.

About the Author

Charlie Buxton is a thirteen year old web creator, author, and songwriter. I made this page for myself to help my author career. I wrote this book when I was 10 and it was illustrated and published at age 13.
